> **How to use this template.** Copy to `docs/architecture/decisions/NNNN-<title>.md`
> with a four-digit sequential number. ADRs record **decisions that constrain
> future work** — pick a stack, adopt a convention, accept a trade-off. They are
> not changelogs and not implementation notes. Keep them short; if you are
> writing pages, you are probably documenting code, not a decision.

## Context

What forces are at play? What is true about the system or team that makes this
decision necessary now? Cite constraints, prior art, or alternatives considered.

## Decision

The decision, in one or two paragraphs. Active voice, present tense:
"We will use X" — not "X seems good."

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or different as a result. Include the painful
parts honestly. If this decision is later reversed, this section is what tells
the next reader why.
